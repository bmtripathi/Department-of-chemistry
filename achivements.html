HOD Desk

The Department of Chemistry at IIT Dharwad is committed to fostering excellence in education, research, and innovation. We strive to provide our students with a strong foundation in chemical sciences while encouraging curiosity, critical thinking, and interdisciplinary learning. Our faculty and researchers are engaged in diverse areas of modern chemistry, creating an intellectually stimulating environment that promotes meaningful scientific contributions.
The department also emphasizes research-driven learning, collaboration, and the development of solutions to contemporary scientific and societal challenges. Through dedicated teaching, active research, and a vibrant academic community, we aim to nurture the next generation of scientists and researchers and contribute to the advancement of chemistry at both national and international levels.

"Best Poster" Award at the 34th CRSI National Symposium in Chemistry

The Chemical Research Society of India (CRSI) is a prestigious platform that brings together leading scientists, industrialists, researchers, and young scholars from across the world to share groundbreaking work in chemistry. Attending the symposium was an enriching experience, with insightful lectures and discussions covering diverse areas of chemistry. I’m truly honored to have my work recognized among such brilliant minds and research. A special thanks to the organizers at IISER Bhopal for hosting such a well-structured and vibrant symposium. A heartfelt thanks to my guide, Prof. Nilkamal Mahanta and Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad. Grateful for this recognition.
