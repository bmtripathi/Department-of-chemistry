Overview of the Department

The Department of Chemistry at IIT Dharwad is a rapidly growing, research-driven unit committed to excellence in teaching, discovery, and innovation. With a strong team of our faculty members, the department today is playing a vital role in the academic and research ecosystem of the country. Our faculties bring diverse expertise spanning organic, inorganic, physical, materials, biological, and computational chemistry, enabling rich interdisciplinary engagement across various disciplines. The department offers rigorous core and elective courses for undergraduate and master’s students, complemented by a PhD program with research opportunities in frontier areas such as photochemistry, materials chemistry, bioinorganic chemistry, spectroscopy, chemical biology, theoretical and computational chemistry powered by artificial intelligence. State-of-the-art experimental laboratories, advanced analytical instruments, access to central instrumentation facilities, and high-performance computing infrastructure empower students and researchers to pursue cutting-edge fundamental and translational research. With active national and international collaborations, various external funding, and a culture of seminars, journal clubs, and outreach activities, the department fosters curiosity, creativity, and impact. Located in the city of Dharwad, the Department of Chemistry at IIT Dharwad not only offers an exciting environment for work but the serene green campus and uniquely pleasant climate of the region provide calmness and boosts individual creativity. At the same time the rapidly growing campus provides a vibrant atmosphere that supports physical and mental development of all working here.
